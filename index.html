The problem 02 / Why

Every day, placement lists, refund tickets and clinic notes get forwarded on email and WhatsApp. Each copy can carry Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards and phone numbers. Once a file is sent, you can't take it back. Andhaar hides the private parts before you hit send.

        Real or fake?
        
It knows a fake when it sees one

        
An order number can look like a card. A roll number can look like an Aadhaar. Andhaar does the math, hides only the real ones, and puts the rest in a "Set aside" list with a plain reason.

        Questions
        08 / FAQ
      

      

        
Good questions

        

          

            Is my file uploaded anywhere?
            
No. Andhaar runs fully inside your browser tab. Your text never goes to our server or anyone else's.

          

          

            Do I need an account?
            
No account, no sign-up, no email. Open the app and start.

          

          

            What does Gemini Nano do here?
            
It finds names and addresses, which simple rules can't catch. It runs on your device in Chrome. If your browser doesn't have it, everything else still works.

          

          

            Can I get the real values back later?
            
Yes. Use the Nicknames style, then save a password-locked key file. With the key and the password, you can bring the original values back.

          

          

            Which files can I use?
            
Plain text (.txt) and spreadsheets saved as .csv, up to 5 MB. You can also just paste text.

          

          

            What if it hides something it shouldn't?
            
Click the mark and choose "Let this through". You can also select any text to hide it yourself.
